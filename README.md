# Supply-Chain-Performance-Dashboard
Dashboard analyzing global supply chain metrics and late delivery bottlenecks
Overview
This dashboard provides a comprehensive, high-level view of supply chain operations and sales performance. As detailed in image_7ca080.png, it is designed to help stakeholders track financial health, monitor delivery efficiency, and analyze sales trends across various departments and global regions.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The top panel displays core metrics for quick health assessment:

Total Sales: 36.78M

Total Profit: 3.97M

Total Orders: 384K

Avg Shipping Days: 3.50

Visualizations & Insights

Sales by Department: A horizontal bar chart highlighting revenue by product category. The "Fan Shop" and "Apparel" departments are the dominant revenue drivers, while "Pet Shop" and "Health and Beauty" show minimal sales volume.

Delivery Status Distribution: A donut chart tracking shipping reliability. It reveals a critical operational bottleneck: 54.83% (98.98K) of orders are classified as "Late delivery." Only 17.84% ship on time, while 23.04% are shipped in advance.

Sales Trend Over Time: A line graph mapping the sum of sales from 2015 to 2018. Sales remained relatively stable between 2015 and 2017 but experienced a sharp, significant decline in 2018.

Sales by Region: A treemap visualizing geographic revenue distribution. Western Europe and Central America represent the largest market shares, followed by South America and Oceania.

Interactive Filters
The right-hand panel includes slicers to manipulate the data views:

Year: Filter the entire dashboard by specific years (2015, 2016, 2017, 2018) to isolate the 2018 drop-off or view historical performance.

Shipping Mode: Filter by service tier (First Class, Same Day, Second Class, Standard Class) to analyze how different shipping methods impact the high rate of late deliveries.
